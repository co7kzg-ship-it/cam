How Tether works

Offline local pairing

  1. On the viewing screen choose Watch here; it displays an animated offer QR.
  2. On the phone choose Send this camera and scan the offer QR.
  3. The phone displays an animated answer QR. On the viewing screen press Scan phone answer and point its camera at the phone.

The offer and answer are transferred by you. The app never contacts a cloud signaling, STUN or TURN server. Media is accepted only over host candidates on the local Wi-Fi.

Controlling the phone from the desktop

LED, autofocus, zoom, resolution and the microphone are all driven from the rail on the right. A control that the phone's camera cannot honour is switched off rather than left to fail silently.

Local network requirement

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the router must allow devices to reach each other. If a VPN is active, enable its local network access or LAN bypass option. There is no internet fallback by design.
