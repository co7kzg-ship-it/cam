FieldCam

Use your phone as a wireless camera. Live feed on your desktop over a direct, encrypted WebRTC link.

  1. On the desktop: choose Watch here — you get a 4-digit code and a QR.
  2. On the phone: tap Scan a viewer QR and point it at the desktop's code, or type the 4 digits.
  3. Tap Go live — the feed goes straight from the phone to the desktop.
  4. No internet or VPN in the way? Use Offline pair on the viewer — a direct local-network link with zero servers, LocalSend-style.

Video/audio never passes through a media server (P2P, DTLS‑SRTP encrypted). The free PeerJS cloud is used only for the initial handshake. Anyone with the code can connect — keep it to yourself.

Help & diagnostics

How it works

  • Viewer: pick Watch here, share the code/link/QR with the phone.
  • Camera: open the link (or type the code) on the phone, tap Go live.
  • The Camera toolbar on the viewer remote-controls the phone (zoom, torch, flip, freeze, quality) when supported.
  • Everything is P2P & encrypted; the PeerJS cloud only performs the handshake (not used at all in Offline pair).
  • Scan (phone): scans the viewer's QR with this app — no need for the stock camera app.
  • Offline pair: works with no internet and VPN on, as long as both devices share the same Wi‑Fi/network.

Tips

  • Mouse-wheel / pinch zooms the viewer image; drag pans when zoomed; double-click toggles 2×.
  • Keep the phone screen on (toggle Keep awake) — iOS pauses cameras in the background.
  • Camera controls (torch, optical zoom, EV) depend on the phone & browser; Android Chrome supports most.
  • Nothing connects? Both devices need internet; strict networks may need the TURN relay included here.
