How Tether works

Pairing

  1. On the desktop choose Watch here — a four-digit code and a QR appear.
  2. Point the phone's camera at the square: it opens this page there with the code already in it.
  3. Tap Connect and allow the camera.

The relay only swaps the two handshake messages. The picture always travels straight from the phone to this browser.

Controls

The rail on the right drives the phone: LED, focus, zoom, resolution, bitrate and the microphone. Every press is answered by the phone, so a camera that cannot do something says so.

If pairing fails

A VPN is the usual reason: turn on allow local network access (also called LAN bypass or split tunnelling), or switch off keep the video on this Wi-Fi in Settings to let it go the long way round. Chrome also hides this machine's local address until the page holds a media permission, which is why the microphone is asked for once and released immediately.
