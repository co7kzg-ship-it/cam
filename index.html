How Tether works

Pairing

  1. On the desktop choose Watch here. A four-digit code and a QR appear.
  2. Scan the QR with the phone, or open the same page there and type the code.
  3. Tap Connect on the phone and allow the camera.

The relay used to swap the two handshake messages never carries the picture. Once the handshake is done the two devices talk to each other directly.

Controlling the phone from the desktop

LED, autofocus, zoom, resolution and the microphone are all driven from the rail on the right. A control that the phone's camera cannot honour is switched off rather than left to fail silently.

Working with a VPN

Two devices on one Wi-Fi pair through their local addresses. Most VPN apps have a switch called allow local network access, LAN bypass or split tunnelling — with that on, pairing behaves as if the VPN were not there. With it off the tunnel swallows local traffic and no direct route exists. Then either:

If the status line says paired, but no route, the code did its job and only the video path is blocked. Switch local network access on in the VPN app, or turn off keep the video on this Wi-Fi in Settings so it can go the long way round.
