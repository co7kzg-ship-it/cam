How Tether works

Pairing

  1. On the viewing screen choose Watch here.
  2. On the phone choose Send this camera and scan the QR once.
  3. The phone connects and its camera appears automatically.

The short signaling handshake is automatic. Video and audio still travel directly between the devices on local Wi-Fi.

Controlling the phone from the desktop

LED, autofocus, zoom, resolution and the microphone are all driven from the rail on the right. A control that the phone's camera cannot honour is switched off rather than left to fail silently.

Local network requirement

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the router must allow devices to reach each other. If a VPN is active, enable its local network access or LAN bypass option.
